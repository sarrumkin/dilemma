ID: ambition_vs_belonging
Comment: Same conflict structure: choose a path with higher status, growth, or achievement, or protect relationships, home routines, and belonging. Topics are intentionally different so lexical overlap is weak.
Intent: A good conflict-based method should group these together even when the surface topic changes.

Raw text: Should I accept the promotion or keep the calmer role?
Option 1: Accept the promotion
Benefits:
  + It supports growth, status, and a stronger role in a concrete career and family time decision.
  + It can create a stronger career path.
  + It gives the choice a stronger sense that ambition is worth the cost.
Costs:
  - It weakens closeness, stable routines, and relationships that also matters here.
  - It may put pressure on evenings with my family.
  - It could make the relational option harder to protect.
Option 2: Keep the calmer role
Benefits:
  + It protects closeness, stable routines, and relationships in this career and family time situation.
  + It keeps evenings with my family safer and more predictable.

Costs:
